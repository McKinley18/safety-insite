## 2. What the service is

Safety InSite is workplace safety inspection software. HazLenz is its governed safety-analysis
system: it analyses observations you record, identifies potential hazards, surfaces conditions it
cannot resolve, supports regulatory reasoning against a governed knowledge base, and recommends
controls and actions.

HazLenz is **decision support**. It is not a safety authority. Its analyses are inputs to your
professional judgement and never a substitute for it.

## 3. Supported intended use

The service is offered for use by qualified safety professionals, in the course of their work,
to record and analyse workplace safety observations at sites for which they hold responsibility.

You must not use the service:

* as the sole basis for determining that a workplace, task or condition is safe;
* to satisfy any obligation that law or regulation assigns to a competent or qualified person,
  except as one input to that person's own assessment;
* in any emergency, imminent-danger or life-safety situation requiring immediate action;
* for any purpose outside your own organisation's safety work.

* detect every hazard present in an observation, a task, or a workplace;

* be available, uninterrupted, or free of defects.

**These are not boilerplate.** Each corresponds to a measured limitation recorded in

## 5. Human review is required, and the software is built to require it

You remain responsible for every workplace decision you make. Specifically:

* Every HazLenz finding requires review by a qualified person before it is relied upon.
* HazLenz does not finalise findings on its own authority and cannot certify a workplace as safe.
* Where HazLenz identifies a condition it cannot resolve, it will say so and route the decision to
  a person rather than resolve it itself. **This is intended behaviour**, not a malfunction, and you
  must not treat an unresolved condition as an absence of hazard.
* The service may refuse to produce an analysis. A refusal means no analysis was produced. It does
  not mean the observation is safe.
