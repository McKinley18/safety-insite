## 1. What we collect

| category | what it is | where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | name, email address, hashed password, role | you, at account creation |
| Organisation data | organisation name, sites, membership and entitlements | you and your organisation's administrator |
| Inspection observations | the safety observations you record, in your own words | you |
| Photographs and documents | evidence images and files you attach to an inspection | you |
| HazLenz analyses | findings, hazard identifications, regulatory references, recommended controls, unresolved conditions, and the reasoning basis recorded for each | generated by the service from your observations |
| Review and override decisions | whether a person confirmed, changed or rejected a finding, the rationale given, and who did it | you |
| Reports | generated inspection reports | generated by the service |
| Audit and security logs | authentication events, record access, file retrieval and deletion, entitlement changes | generated automatically |
| Diagnostic information | request timing, HTTP status, error events, client IP address, user agent | generated automatically by our hosting platform |
| Billing data | subscription and payment status | Stripe (we do not receive or store card numbers) |

**Sensitivity, stated plainly.** Inspection observations are free text written by you. They can name
individual workers, describe their conduct, and record injuries or unsafe acts. Nothing in the
product prevents this, and the service has no way to detect it. Please record only what your
organisation's own policies permit, and prefer role descriptions over names.

## 4. We do not train models on your content

Your operational content is **not** used to train third-party AI models, and not used to train any
future model of ours. Submitting an observation for analysis does not license it for training. The
full boundary, including feedback and corrections, is in
[`FEEDBACK_AND_IMPROVEMENT_BOUNDARY.md`](FEEDBACK_AND_IMPROVEMENT_BOUNDARY.md).

**One honest limit.** Our own systems do not use your content for training, and we have configured
nothing that would. We have **not** completed a contractual review of our AI provider's data
retention and training terms — that review is item 8 of the launch gate in
`docs/PRODUCT-CLAIMS-REGISTER.md` and it is `NOT_YET_EXECUTED`. We therefore do **not** claim
zero-retention by the provider, because we have not verified it. Treat observation text you submit
for analysis as disclosed to a third-party processor under that processor's terms.

## 5. How long we keep it

**During the beta, we do not operate automated deletion.** There is no retention sweeper and no
expiry job. Records persist until the beta ends or until you ask us to delete them. This is a
deliberate beta posture and it is stated here rather than implied away.

| data | behaviour |
|---|---|
| Files you delete in the product | the stored file is **actually deleted** from object storage. A metadata record — that a file existed, who deleted it and when — is retained for the audit trail |
| Inspections, analyses, reports | retained for the beta term |
| Audit and security logs | retained for the beta term; these are the integrity record and are not deleted on request except where law requires |
| Diagnostic logs | retained by our hosting platform under its own retention window, which we do not control |
| Everything else | deleted or returned at the end of the beta, on your instruction |

**Two implementation limits you should know.** Only the person who uploaded a file can delete it
through the product — a colleague in the same organisation cannot. And deletion of other record
types is performed by us on request rather than by a self-service control, because no such control
exists yet.

## 6. Security

Verified at §269 against the running production system:

* All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS, HSTS enforced).
* The database connection requires TLS.
* Object storage is private. Unsigned and unauthenticated requests are refused, and knowing a
  file's identifier confers no access — every retrieval is authorised against your account and
  organisation first.
* Stored files are integrity-checked on retrieval; a file that does not match its recorded checksum
  is not served.
* Access between organisations is enforced server-side on every request.
* Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text.
* Development authentication bypasses are disabled in production, and the application refuses to
  start if they are enabled.
* Access to your data is logged.

No system is perfectly secure, and this is a beta.
