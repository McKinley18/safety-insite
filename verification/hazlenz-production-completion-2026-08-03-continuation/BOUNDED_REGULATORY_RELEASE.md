# Bounded release

The only defensible current definitive release is the pre-existing governed 19-standard set: 13 OSHA records and 6 MSHA records with approved runtime eligibility. Imported OSHA/MSHA documents remain searchable only within review/diagnostic workflows and cannot become direct citations. OSHA Part 1904, broad coal-mine coverage, and unapproved imported families are outside the definitive release scope.
