4. SIMULTANEOUS-FEASIBILITY PROOF

   Necessary condition, by inclusion-exclusion over ONE selected row set R:
     H >= |R| >= |OWED u FORBIDDEN| = |OWED| + |FORBIDDEN| - |OWED n FORBIDDEN|
     => |OWED n FORBIDDEN| >= O + F - H

     O = CLARIFICATION_OWED minimum                 20
     F = FORBIDDEN_FAMILY_NEGATIVE_CONTROL minimum  48
     H = COHORT_SIZE_POLICY.hardCeiling             60
     REQUIRED overlap  O + F - H                  = 8

     AVAILABLE overlap in the OPEN pool           = 3   (AUG-10, AUG-11, SEM-30)
     AVAILABLE overlap INCLUDING offsets 2 and 3  = 3   (unchanged: those partitions carry no gap label)

   MAXIMUM achievable FORBIDDEN count in any 60-row cohort that also carries 20 OWED rows:
     overlap 3 + min(H - O = 40, non-OWED forbidden available 112) = 43
     required 48   =>  SHORT BY 5

   FEASIBLE = false

   OPENING THE RESERVE CANNOT CHANGE THIS. Offsets 2 and 3 add only NOT-OWED forbidden
   rows, and the binding term is the OWED-AND-FORBIDDEN overlap, to which they contribute
   ZERO -- the artifact carries no gap label at all.

   THE VERDICT IS ROBUST TO THE RESERVED-COUNT UNCERTAINTY. Only `min(H - O, non-OWED
   forbidden) matters, and the slot count H - O = 40 is already the
   binding side WITHOUT the reserve: 41 non-OWED forbidden rows are already
   available in the open pool (112 including the reserve upper bound), and
   both exceed 40. The maximum is therefore 43 either way.

   Under COHORT_SIZE_POLICY.onInsufficiency the correct action is to STOP and report the
   exact reason, with the reserve INTACT.
