# Phase 5 — Counterfactual matrix

Each variant is classified on the same four axes. **Six of the nine already exist as frozen
fixtures**, which is itself a finding: the boundary this adjudication is being asked to draw has
largely been drawn already, and the existing classifications are consistent with the R6 oracle.

Classification vocabulary: **current candidate** / **clarification** / **contextual fact**
(true, stated, non-actionable — belongs in the summary at most) / **future prerequisite** /
**no finding**.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R6-A** | *(none — exact frozen R6)* | `R6` | **NO** | **NO** | guard is off; energy verified at zero | guard reinstatement before re-energization |
| **R6-B** | machine merely stopped, not isolated | **`V5`** | **YES** — `lockout_tagout` | yes (either carrier) | — | — |
| **R6-C** | isolation incomplete | **`V4`** (second live source), **`U-C`** | **YES** — `lockout_tagout` | yes | — | — |

| **R6-F** | capable of automatic/unexpected restart | **`V6`** | **YES** — `machine_guarding` | yes | — | — |

## Coherence of the boundary across all nine

The classifications are generated by a single rule, applied uniformly:

> **A guard that is off is a current machine-guarding hazard exactly when the observation states
> a live pathway to harm** — either hazardous motion is possible (`R6-B/C/D/E/F`, `R6-I`) or a
> person is stated to be in contact range of the exposed point (`R6-G`/`V7`) — **and it is a
> future prerequisite when the only pathway is a transition the observation does not state is
> occurring** (`R6-A`).

`R6-H` is the hinge that proves the rule is not merely "LOTO silences guarding." The physical
state in `R6-H` is *identical* to `R6-A` — guard off, energy still isolated, no motion yet
possible. What changes is that the observation now states the transition is imminent, which
makes the restoration prerequisite **due now**. It becomes a genuinely decision-critical
clarification (*"has the guard been reinstalled before this restart?"*) — the very question that
is *not* decision-critical in `R6-A`, asked at the one moment it changes an outcome.

`R6-I` is the plain 1910.212 case with no LOTO left standing: motion is available, the guard is
absent, and both the deterministic predicate (`energySafe = false`, `guardPresent = false` →
`notApplicable = false`) and the Expert layer should call it.

## The two gaps this exposes in the frozen corpus

**`R6-H` and `R6-I` do not exist as fixtures.** They are the only two variants in the matrix
without one, and they are precisely the two that separate a *future restoration prerequisite*
from a *current guarding hazard* — the exact distinction the last three prompt generations have
been fighting over.

That is a real, measurable gap. Every existing counterfactual (`V1`–`V8`) flips a fact about
**energy control**; not one flips a fact about the **restoration transition**. The corpus can
therefore demonstrate that the model does not under-call an uncontrolled energy state, but it
cannot demonstrate that the model correctly *locates* the restoration obligation in time — which
is the specific reasoning error under adjudication.

Adding `R6-H` and `R6-I` would make the current oracle falsifiable in the direction it is
currently untested: a rule that suppressed R6 by teaching "guard-off under LOTO is never
anything" would pass every existing fixture and **fail `R6-H` and `R6-I`.** Recommended in
PHASE7/8 as fixture-corpus work, not implemented here.

## Note on `U-F`

`U-F` (§113/§114, *"legitimate future-transition clarification"*) is adjacent to `R6-H` but not
a substitute: it passed hosted with the load-bearing question *"Is the area exhaust fan
currently running?"* — a **present-state** question about a conditional hazard, and both its
supporting candidates were correctly marked `UNKNOWN` rather than `ACTIVE`. It demonstrates the
model can handle conditional state well when the deciding fact is a present one. It does not
test a stated imminent transition.
