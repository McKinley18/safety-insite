# §255 — Expert HazLenz Driver-Role Capability Boundary

Zero provider calls. Zero database operations. No remediation. No production code changed.

## The classification

    BOUNDED EXPERT CAPABILITY LIMIT —
    AUTONOMOUS DRIVER-ROLE ASSIGNMENT NOT ACCEPTED FOR v1.0

The six-case frozen experiment is **CLOSED**. It will not be redrawn, rescored, extended by a seventh
case, or reopened by a prompt, a field, a validator or a micro-probe.

## What the measurement established

| | |
|---|---|
| Frozen acceptance floor | 5 / 6 |
| Role-presence coherence, admission-aware | **2 / 6** |
| Role-presence coherence, raw output, admission ignored | **4 / 6** |

Contained refusals are not successes and excessive caution is not semantic correctness. The
capability is not accepted.

## The six cases, preserved

**H1 — correct.** The barriered-off guardrail gap was read as controlled and left at CONTINUE.

**H2 — correct, and field evidence that the §253 repair operates as intended.** A real cessation
driver reached STOP, and `alongsideControlConsidered` was written as text: *"gas testing current and
a top-man posted"*. The confrontation §253 made the only expressible form actually happened.

**H3 — self-consistency failure.** The analysis declared an unresolved fact and then referenced it
nowhere in the operational posture.

**H4 — self-reference failure plus semantic over-restriction.** The discharging control did not
satisfy the required exact self-reference, and separately the analysis restricted beyond what the
frozen case truth justifies.

**H5 — genuine role-selection failure.** Continuation-controlling significance was assigned to an
unresolved role although the analysis's own candidate state established the condition as ACTIVE.

**H6 — genuine and decision-significant role-selection failure.** Against a frozen truth of a
non-controlling follow-up on an operation permitted to continue, the analysis assigned
continuation-controlling significance to two entries, omitted the required follow-up role, selected
HOLD, and supplied a fluent structured justification for the incorrect allocation. The output was
structurally valid and passed admission.

## The H6 architectural finding

> **A model-generated structured justification for a driver role does not establish that the role it
> justifies is semantically correct.**

The failure on H6 is not an absence of explanation. An explanation was present, fluent, and wrong.
More justification prose, another justification field, another prompt reminder or another semantic
self-certification mechanism would each answer a problem the evidence says we do not have.

## Safety interpretation

**This is a capability failure, not an unsafe product escape.** No observed case under-restricted the
frozen truth, allowed unsafe continuation, fabricated semantic support admitted as established fact,
improperly settled an unresolved property, produced an authority escape, or let an unsafe operational
recommendation escape containment. Every miss was in the over-restrictive direction.

That distinction must stay explicit, and it does not make the limit harmless. Excessive restriction
unnecessarily stops permitted work, reduces user trust, creates avoidable operational friction, and
teaches users to disregard HazLenz over time. It is a real product defect in a different register.

## The v1.0 product boundary

Expert HazLenz is **not** authorized to independently settle driver-role assignment where the product
consequence depends on distinguishing **continuation-controlling** from **follow-up / non-controlling**.

HazLenz may still produce the underlying analysis: observed condition, hazards, unresolved facts,
controls and control relationships, candidate operational posture, corrective action, evidence
requirements, regulatory reasoning, standards and explanation.

The final role distinction must be **human-confirmed** wherever it materially determines whether work
stops, holds, continues with controls, or continues while a separate follow-up remains open.

### The confirmation must stay narrow

The intended experience is preserved: limited user input, HazLenz performs the analysis, the user
reviews only consequential conclusions. The user must not reconstruct the analysis and must not
populate a technical role taxonomy. Where material ambiguity exists, HazLenz presents its conclusion
and the relevant facts and asks for confirmation of the operational classification — in substance,
*"HazLenz identified this unresolved item as controlling whether work may continue. Confirm or
change."*

### Fail-closed until confirmed

Until confirmation occurs, an uncertain driver-role classification must not become authoritative
operational settlement. No semantic fact is invented, confirmation is never inferred, and the absence
of confirmation is never treated as evidence that a role is controlling or non-controlling.

**§255 authorizes the boundary, not the implementation.** The mechanism belongs to a later bounded
product-integration section.

## Capability claim boundary

Do not claim that Expert HazLenz autonomously and reliably determines which unresolved facts are
continuation-controlling versus follow-up in all supported situations.

Permissible characterization:

> HazLenz analyzes operational safety conditions and identifies unresolved issues and potential
> work-posture consequences, with consequential operational classifications subject to user review
> where required.

## Reopening rule

Autonomous driver-role capability is not reopened by a better prompt, another role field, another
deterministic check or another small hosted cohort.

It reopens only after a **material intelligence or architecture change** that plausibly changes
semantic role-selection capability — a materially different reasoning architecture, HazLenz-owned
learned role reasoning, materially stronger context and state representation, a specialised
independently validated semantic reasoner, or another substantial successor capability absent from
Candidate v2.3.

If reopened, a new acceptance instrument is designed from first principles. **§254 may never be
reused as a tuning set.**

## Known non-blocking gap, carried forward

The `dischargingControlRef` transmitted-null / projection-refusal inconsistency remains an unresolved
contract-consistency gap. It was not materially encountered by the six observations, it did not cause
the observed role failure, and it must not be conflated with the capability result. It is resolved
separately when product sequencing justifies it.
