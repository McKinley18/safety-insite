# HazLenz Gap Analysis

## Competing truth systems

The production endpoint calls a weighted classifier, risk evaluator, native reasoning orchestrator, knowledge router/shards, database-backed applicable-standards service, expert applicability rules, citation recovery/ranking, action engine, and optional full intelligence orchestrator. Later layers repair or overwrite earlier output. The controller itself can recover a primary citation into visible arrays. This makes it difficult to identify one authoritative decision.

## Evidence is text-first, not fact-first

Negation, temporal state, worker report versus direct observation, control state, and contradictions are converted back into fused text. Regex/ranking logic then repeatedly reinterprets that text. The eight safe-state failures and contradiction-domain failures are the expected consequence.

## Jurisdiction is both supplied and inferred

Scope aliases (`osha_general`, `osha_general_industry`, display forms) coexist. Jurisdiction is inferred from text and supplied through structured observation, while mine-type logic later filters parts. Ambiguous facilities can therefore leak jurisdiction-specific candidates before applicability is resolved.

## Citation recovery bypasses sufficiency

Recovery and API-boundary repair are designed to prevent empty output, but emptiness is often the correct safety behavior when evidence is insufficient. This creates pressure toward unsupported standards and contributed to prohibited-family promotions.

## Corpus/runtime mismatch

The broad reasoning code assumes a deep authoritative corpus; the live database has 19 standards and eight chunks. Static seeds, rules, supplemental guidance, knowledge packs, and database rows compete, so citation hydration and source authority are not dependable.

## Risk and actions are generated too early

Risk is calculated immediately after the first classifier result, before final applicability and contradiction handling. Actions are generated before the optional full intelligence pass. Later evidence gates do not necessarily invalidate the earlier risk/action.

## Confidence has no empirical meaning

Confidence is recalibrated in multiple components and expressed as fractions, percentages, and bands. No held-out expert dataset demonstrates that a reported 80% result is correct approximately 80% of the time.

## Monolithic orchestration impairs operations

`safescope-v2.service.ts` contains thousands of lines of inline regex, output repair, clarification, ranking, and compatibility logic. The isolated process used 866 MB at startup and approximately 845 MB during sustained evaluation.
