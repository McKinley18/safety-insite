## Selected migration (Phase 4/5)

Two live, meaningful, low-risk consumers now build/consume the same shared fact representation from `backend/src/safescope-v2/evidence/shared-evidence-facts.ts`:

1. **`evidence-foundation.ts`** — refactored from defining its own `extract()`/`fact()`/`has()`/`ids()` inline to importing `buildEvidenceFacts()`/`hasFact()`/`factIds()` from the shared module. Pure move, zero behavior change (proven via differential output testing, see `V5_C02_VERIFICATION.md`).
2. **`evidence-sufficiency-core/evidence-sufficiency.service.ts`** (`EvidenceSufficiencyService`, live via the orchestrator) — now also receives the shared fact array (built once by the orchestrator from the same `fusedText` already in scope) and attaches it as new, purely additive `evidenceFactTrace` provenance on its output. No existing field (`factScores`, `sufficiencyLevel`, `confidenceImpact`, `missingCriticalFacts`, etc.) is read from or written by the shared facts in this phase — see `V5_C02_SHARED_FACT_CONTRACT.md` for why this scope was chosen over a deeper (decision-affecting) migration.

All other live consumers identified above were evaluated and intentionally **not** migrated in C02, per the task's explicit instruction not to force unsafe wiring: each is either (a) a heavily hand-tuned, precision-sensitive regex surface whose 228/228-fixture recognition record this task explicitly protects, (b) already one layer downstream of raw text (operates on structured categorical fields, not `fusedText` itself), or (c) a multi-hop live chain whose full behavioral surface could not be characterized and safely diffed within this phase's effort budget.
