# Offline behavior

The evidence/applicability foundation is deterministic and network-independent. Focused offline tests passed for:

- machine-guard evidence evaluation;
- unavailable optional pack handling;
- stale-pack labeling;
- prohibited definitive language when authority is unavailable.

The response records offline bundle identity/version and does not promote unreviewed Internet material. Connected retrieval failure falls back to the versioned local behavior rather than fabricating text.

Important limitation: the disposable database's `standards_master` contains only eight hydrated standards. The foundation covers more applicability families but does not invent quotation text for missing entries. Completing the versioned official federal corpus and its audited ingestion is the recommended next phase.
