## Life-safety recall and classification

### Energy control and electrical

- C007 explicitly establishes energized servicing and missing personal isolation. It was reduced to generic electrical condition and ranked 56.12004 over 56.12016.
- C022 explicitly establishes exposed copper, wet conductive surroundings, and direct contact. A related but non-primary electrical citation outranked the required family.
- C037 explicitly establishes reachable energized bus bars but “cover/guard” language caused machine-guarding classification.
- C085 explicitly establishes crane operation, energized voltage, six-foot proximity, and absent encroachment controls. Lifting classification did not trigger the power-line family.

Systemic correction: extract equipment/activity/energy/exposure facts before classification; evaluate family predicates independently of the single selected category; allow multiple hazard mechanisms; rank direct predicates above lexical category similarity.

### Ground, excavation, and suspended loads

- C010 “drumming/loose slabs above active underground travelway” was flattened into walking surfaces.
- C064 trench geometry and absent protective system were mistaken for confined space.
- C073 suspended load and people beneath it were mistaken for electrical.

Systemic correction: represent geometry, suspended-load state, overhead exposure, excavation depth/type, and protective systems as typed facts; use life-safety candidate generators that do not depend on the primary text class.

### Egress

- C055 correctly classified emergency egress but failed to retrieve/promote the direct locked-exit family.

Systemic correction: deterministic direct-family candidate generation from supported predicates; authoritative citation hydration must follow, never fabricated text.
