Holdout review themes:

- incomplete closure after a relevant clarification;
- candidate-only egress, power-line, fall-zone, and guarding determinations;
- ergonomics where no direct standard should be overstated;
- confined-space context without all classification facts;
- missing hydrated standard family (including housekeeping);
- answered clarification cases where scoring expects a fuller post-answer conclusion.

These are not hidden inside averages. Per-case expected criteria, actual outputs, classifications, and reasons are in the two result JSON files.
