# Defect taxonomy

- `CORRECTIVE_GENERIC`: generic permanent-control fallback replaced by domain-aware evidence-bound controls.
- `CORRECTIVE_CROSS_FAMILY`: walking-surface observation received electrical action; indicates upstream domain/context association weakness still open.
- `MECHANISM_RESTATEMENT`: causal output used opaque or weak mechanism wording; humanized causal templates added.
- `MECHANISM_CONTEXT_MISMATCH`: some outputs still combine a correct family with an incorrect mechanism/activity and require upstream scenario-intelligence investigation.
- `STANDARD_UNDERDEVELOPED`: standards remain candidate-oriented and need a clean throttled measurement before modification.
- `TEMPORAL_STATE_LIMITATION`: corrected/reported conditions remain conservative and require separate state-quality corpus evaluation.
