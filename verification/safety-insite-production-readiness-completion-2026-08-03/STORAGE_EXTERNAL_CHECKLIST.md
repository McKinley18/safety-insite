# Live storage external checklist

Required: non-local S3-compatible credentials and private bucket. Verify upload, SHA-256, content type/size metadata, owner and organization keys, authorized retrieval, 404/403 unauthorized denial, duplicate upload semantics, retry/provider failure, signed URL expiry, delete/lifecycle policy, report and evidence objects, public-access block, encryption, CORS, retention, and cross-tenant isolation. Local `local_test` evidence is not production proof.
