## The three failures, and they are not the same failure

**B1 — a §237 contract-binding defect, found by the confirmation that was meant to close the
phase.** The model put the controlling role on both its declaration *and* on a hazard candidate it
had itself marked INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. §237 bound every UNRESOLVED role to declarations only, on
the reasoning that an unresolved fact is by definition not established. That reasoning is right
about declarations and wrong about candidates: the §210J condition-state vocabulary contains

candidate. The model made a coherent statement the contract had no member for. **The semantic
judgment was correct.**

**B2 — the model's own labels disagree, and refusing is the contract working.** The same shape, but
the candidate was marked ACTIVE, meaning established and present, and then labelled an unresolved
property controlling continuation. Those two cannot both be true. This must not be counted as the
same defect as B1, and the posture and declaration-level role were still correct.

**C1 — posture-object coherence, and it is the §234 E2 finding recurring.** The model reached
CONTINUE_WITH_CONTROLS with the right controls and *also* wrote the corrective action into the
resume condition, under a posture whose schema says both resume lists must be empty. The rule that
caught it is the one §235 added and §237 retained, doing exactly what it was added for.
