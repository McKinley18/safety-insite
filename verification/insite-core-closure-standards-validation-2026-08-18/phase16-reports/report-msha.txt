Detailed Findings
Finding 1 — Machine Guarding
WHAT WAS OBSERVED
the guard on the secondary crusher conveyor tail pulley is missing
FULL SOURCE OBSERVATION (SHARED ACROSS THIS INSPECTION’S FINDINGS)
The guard on the secondary crusher conveyor tail pulley is missing, exposing miners walking the travelway to the moving parts.
Manual finding: a miner is servicing the screen deck drive with power connected and no lock or tag applied.
RISK
 CRITICAL
Severity 4 · Likelihood 4 · Score 16

Accepted — Reviewed against the observed facts; accepted as an advisory conclusion.
RECOMMENDED CORRECTIVE ACTION
Verify and correct reviewed condition 1
Immediate: Keep the equipment out of service and restrict access to the point of operation.
Permanent: Install or restore guarding that prevents access to the moving hazard during operation and foreseeable
tasks.
Verification: Test the guard and document that access to the moving hazard is prevented.
Status: Open · Priority: Urgent
