Finding 1 — Electrical
WHAT WAS OBSERVED
cord
FULL SOURCE OBSERVATION (SHARED ACROSS THIS INSPECTION’S FINDINGS)
A roofer is working within four feet of an unprotected roof edge about 18 feet above grade with no guardrail or personal fall arrest
system in use. Manual finding: the extension cord powering the circular saw at the deck edge has exposed copper conductors and
remains energized while crew members are working around it.
RISK
  HIGH
APPLICABLE STANDARD
Not established for this specific finding.
QUALIFIED-PERSON REVIEW
Accepted — Reviewed against the observed facts; accepted as an advisory conclusion.
RECOMMENDED CORRECTIVE ACTION
Electrical corrective action
Immediate: Isolate the affected extension cord from service and route damaged electrical-integrity concerns for
qualified review before reuse.
Permanent: Replace damaged wiring assemblies with rated replacement components appropriate for the affected
equipment and environment to ensure continuous, safe electrical-integrity barriers.
Verification: Perform a formal inspection and functional verification by a competent person before removing interim
controls or resuming normal operations.
