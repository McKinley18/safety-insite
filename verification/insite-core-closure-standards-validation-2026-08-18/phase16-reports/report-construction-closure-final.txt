container of solvent in the paint room has no label identifying its contents or hazards. Manual finding: the extension cord powering
the chop saw has exposed copper conductors and remains energized where laborers are working.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
29 CFR 1926.34(a) — Means of egress - General
HazLenz standard summary: In every building or structure on a construction site, exits must be so arranged and maintained

1926.34(a)); means of egress must be continually maintained free of all obstructions or impediments to full instant use in
case of fire or other emergency (1926.34(c)). HazLenz basis: Supported by submitted evidence for OSHA Construction
means of egress; qualified review remains required.
