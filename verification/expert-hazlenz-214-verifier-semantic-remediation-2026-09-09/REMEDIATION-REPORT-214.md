## 3. R-V1 — THE ROLE TEST

§212's test asked what you would need to know "if you could see the workplace exactly as it is".
§213 T1 shows exactly how that failed:

> a sound-looking weld is consistent with both an untested weld and a tested-and-passed one, so the
> act of testing … is the property, not something visible on inspection

True about **looking at** a weld, false about **knowing** its condition. The counterfactual was
sound and the phrasing invited the wrong reading, so the phrasing is gone rather than supplemented.

The successor removes the artifact instead of granting sight:

> WITH THAT TEST OR RECORD OUT OF THE PICTURE ENTIRELY, COULD THE UNDERLYING CONDITION STILL BE
> EITHER SATISFACTORY OR ADVERSE ON ITS OWN?

**YES** — the condition is the property. *A weld is sound or unsound whether or not anyone crack
tested it. A bund holds or leaks whether or not the certificate is in the folder.* Those are T1's
and T2's mechanisms, carried by mechanism rather than by replay. The block adds, in terms, *do not
answer this by asking whether LOOKING would settle it — a great many conditions are invisible and
are still conditions.*

**NO, because carrying out the act is itself what is required** — the act is the property, with
three worked examples, followed immediately by the unchanged "you must not spend it" paragraph.

It is a semantic role test. No vocabulary is banned anywhere.

## 4. R-V2 — THREE WORLDS

§213 T10 verified as-is a `branchB` reading *"the suspension is **unproven**, or does not carry the
truss"*, and T1 affirmed *"unconfirmed or inadequate"* as a genuine partition. Both let "not
established" sit inside the adverse branch.

The block now names the three worlds and assigns each field to one: branchA is the satisfactory
world, branchB the adverse one, and what is done meanwhile belongs to the unresolved one **and to
nothing else**. It states that the third world is not a third answer.

The concern entry now names the phrases §213 missed — *not confirmed, not documented, not measured,
unproven, certificate missing, test absent* — and the **"or" that smuggles one in beside the real
adverse state**, which is the exact construction both failures used. It carries the counterfactual:

> COULD THE CONDITION ACTUALLY BE FINE EVEN THOUGH NOBODY CAN SHOW IT?

Semantic and model-authored. Nothing implements it deterministically, and the suite asserts that.

**The exception is stated in the same list item**, so the fix does not become the next
overcorrection: where the absence itself is the substantive adverse state — no permit raised, no
examination ever done — an absence-shaped branchB is correct and must be left alone.

## 5. R-V3 — FACT SCOPE

§213 T6 bound both targets correctly and then nominated the sibling. Not target-key corruption;
fact-scope expansion.

**Instruction half.** The block permits conversational awareness explicitly — *say so in your
reasoning if it helps a reader, nobody is asking you to pretend you did not notice* — and refuses
the structured route: do not raise a different fact, and do not offer a question that settles a
different one in place of one that settles this one.

**Deterministic half, and it is structural rather than semantic.** The §212 request supplies one
owed fact and does not ask for linked-fact validation, so a structured nomination is out of scope by
the **shape of the request**, not by the meaning of its contents. The rule reads six inputs: the
supplied-key count, a `multiFactValidationRequested` flag, the target key, the presence of a
nomination, closed-set membership of the source mode, and exact string equality on declared keys. It
reads no prose at all.

The rule is **contract-driven**: when a future contract genuinely asks for linked facts it sets the
flag and the rule stands down. That path is exercised.

The §213 T6 shape — correct binding plus a nomination — is now refused with
`NOMINATION_OUTSIDE_TARGET_SCOPE`.

### The demonstration

A blanket vocabulary rule was run over the fixture set as a **negative control**: challenge any
property mentioning a test, check, inspection, record, certificate, examination, proof load or
permit. It is the exact rule the authorization forbids, it is unreachable from any request builder
or admission path, and the suite asserts that.

It gets **4 of 12 wrong**, and wrong on exactly one member of each vocabulary pair:

```
A2_EXAMINATION_AS_THE_PROPERTY
B2_PROOF_LOAD_AS_THE_PROPERTY
C2F_ABSENCE_IS_THE_ADVERSE_STATE
E1_PROCESS_COMPLETED_STATE_MAY_REMAIN
```

That is the point of the pairing: **no word list can pass both members of a pair.** Only semantic
role can.
