  checkContradiction   lexical presence used to REJECT.  A false positive DELETES a correct finding.
  checkStateSupported  lexical presence used to ADMIT a state the model already claimed. A false
                       positive ACCEPTS A WRONG STATE.

--- checkContradiction, correction/removal path (5 reproduced) ---
  D-FLD-175       'discarded' as an ATTRIBUTIVE MODIFIER on "conveyor rollers".
                  Asserted predicate is "is blocked". HIGH-CONSEQUENCE electrical finding DELETED.

                  applied"). A negated correction is not a correction. HIGH-CONSEQUENCE LOTO DELETED.
  GUARD-REMOVED   bare 'removed' whose SUBJECT IS THE CONTROL ("the chain guard has been removed").

A 4-cell discriminator matrix varying the two properties INDEPENDENTLY:

  cell   unobserved fact?   is it the DECIDING fact?   expected              measured
   A          yes                  yes                 INSUFF + question     2 of 3 correct
   B          yes                  NO                  ACTIVE, no question   3 of 3 CORRECT
   C          no                   n/a                 ACTIVE, no question   1 of 2 correct
   D          silent               n/a                 INSUFF + question     fixture mislabelled

  >> CELL B IS ALREADY CORRECT, 3 of 3. The model does NOT over-generalise could-not-observe wording.
     The defect is therefore NARROW and TARGETED, which is direct evidence AGAINST any global change
     in clarification pressure.

--- THE ACTUAL MECHANISM, and it contradicts the documented account ---

Section 34.5 and the L3-2d NEXT_ACTION.md described D-NG-04 / D-CR-04 as an OSCILLATION between the
ACTIVE and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE rungs. A clause-position ablation says otherwise:

  NG04-orig                "No flammable atmosphere was detected ..., and the fitter went inside"  -> INSUFFICIENT
  NG04-noNegation          "The atmosphere WAS TESTED ..., and the fitter went inside"             -> INSUFFICIENT
  NG04-secondClauseAlone   "The fitter went inside ..."                                            -> ACTIVE
  NG04-negationLast        "The fitter went inside ..., and no flammable atmosphere was detected"  -> ACTIVE

  CR04-orig                "Two men were on the roof near the edge; I was too far away to see ..."  -> ACTIVE
  CR04-observeFirst        "I was too far away to see whether the two men on the roof were tied off" -> INSUFFICIENT + question

REMOVING THE NEGATION CHANGES NOTHING. MOVING THE CLAUSE CHANGES EVERYTHING.

The mechanism is CLAUSE POSITION: the model classifies the whole observation from its FIRST clause
and under-weights every later one. D-NG-04 and D-CR-04 are therefore not two competing pressures --
they are ONE defect seen from two sides:

  * a reassuring clause first  -> the hazard in clause two is lost      (D-NG-04)
  * a situation clause first   -> the unobserved deciding fact in clause two is lost (D-CR-04)

This is recorded rather than worked around, and it redirects the E2 repair away from clarification
pressure entirely and onto per-clause evaluation.
