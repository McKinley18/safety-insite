# Accessibility and Product Trust Review

The new canonical workspace includes explicit labels, keyboard-native controls, focus rings, minimum-height action targets, `aria-live` status, mobile-responsive layout, and light/dark colors. It explicitly states that HazLenz is advisory and requires qualified review.

The authenticated mobile browser flow passed at 390x844. A full WCAG contrast audit, screen-reader session, keyboard traversal of every legacy page, and automated axe suite were not completed. Legacy inspection pages therefore remain an accessibility risk and are not the approved canonical pilot path.
