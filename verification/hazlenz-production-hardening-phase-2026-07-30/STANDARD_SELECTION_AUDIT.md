# Standard selection audit

The guided contract remains predicate-gated: SUPPORTED may be direct; UNKNOWN remains candidate; contradicted/not-applicable decisions are suppressed. One primary and at most two distinct additional standards are exposed.

This phase added:

- explicit approved versus provisional source status
- confidence limitation when regulatory source approval/coverage is incomplete
- evidence-linked finding candidates
- split-review signaling when multiple distinct supported families exist

No new standard text or definitive family promotion was introduced.
