# Multi-hazard results

The guided contract now emits up to three evidence-linked `findingCandidates` and `multiHazardReview.requiresSplitReview` when more than one distinct family is SUPPORTED. It instructs reviewers to split distinct mechanisms and merge duplicate obligations.

Focused contract tests cover one-family and two-family behavior. Full server-side split/merge persistence and separate per-hazard risk/action editors are not implemented and remain architectural work.
