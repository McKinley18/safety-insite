## What was measured and found sound

* **Governance / cutover posture is correct.** All six real workflows returned **zero** governed keys,
  `knowledgeReleaseId` NULL on every persisted row, LEGACY authority throughout.
* **Report governance is clean.** 7 generated PDFs, **0 hits** on 20 governance/shadow patterns.
* **Negation IS handled correctly where the phrasing is recognised** — A02, B12 (double negative),
  B14 (contractor lockout verified) all classify `Controlled Condition` / `SAFE_VERIFIED` with zero
  citations and `Controlled` risk.
* **Jurisdiction inference is truthful and conservative** — it fires on only 11 of 66 and was correct
  on every obvious case, always labelled `HAZLENZ_INFERRED`, never `USER_CONFIRMED`.
* **The finalization gate works** — "Every current finding requires a completed human review before
  finalization" correctly refused an inspection whose decomposed findings were unreviewed.
* **No new regression.** `test:hazlenz-core` reproduces **28 of 30**, exactly the two documented
  pre-existing failures (§13.1) and no third.
