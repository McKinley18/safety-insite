## Why G3 is load-bearing

G3 is chosen so that **perfect direct sight of the physical condition does not settle it**. You can
see exactly where the other crane stands, that its driver is in the cab, and that the walkway is
clear, and the decision still turns on whether that driver was told to stay off the runway, because
he can move at any moment. Under GATE 12's own property-selection question the act is therefore the
property.

It fails if GATE 12 became a ban on process language (`G3.Q2`), a global preference for
physical-state properties (`G3.Q3`), or a global hedging requirement (`G3.Q4`). All three are frozen
mandatory on axis H.
