# Regulatory scope

The implemented reference import scope is:

- OSHA General Industry: 29 CFR Part 1910 source list configured by the official importer
- OSHA Construction: 29 CFR Part 1926 source list configured by the official importer
- MSHA: configured 30 CFR Parts 46, 47, 48, 50, 56, 57, 62, 75, and 77

This is not yet the marketed production scope. The imported material is Tier 3 searchable reference until approved. OSHA recordkeeping Part 1904 is not part of the completed import. Broad coal-mine applicability is not approved. The UI and deployment documentation must not claim comprehensive federal coverage until reconciliation and human approval are complete.

Tier policy:

1. Approved deterministic applicability may be direct.
2. Approved candidate applicability may be candidate-only.
3. Searchable official reference may not be presented as an applicable violation.
4. Ingested/unreviewed content cannot influence definitive conclusions.
