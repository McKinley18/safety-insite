# Verified component freeze

Treat persistence, finding review governance, authorization/entitlement, report snapshot immutability, report concurrency/idempotency, and state-aware unsupported-promotion prevention as protected contracts. Their prior artifacts and regressions are the basis for the VERIFIED_STABLE designation.

Modification is allowed only after a reproducible defect is traced to the component, a focused regression is added, and the relevant closed-gate suite is rerun. Mechanism quality, corrective actions, frontend projection, and knowledge coverage remain active development areas and are not frozen.
