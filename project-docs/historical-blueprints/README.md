# Historical blueprints and transition prompts

**These documents are historical. They do not describe the current system.**

They are the planning and transition material written while the product was moving from its earlier
SafeScope / Sentinel Safety naming to Safety InSite and HazLenz. They are kept because they record
why the architecture is shaped the way it is, and they retain the retired brand names because that
is what those documents actually said.

For the current state, read instead:

- `docs/hazlenz/current/EXPERT_HAZLENZ_CURRENT_STATE.md` — what exists now
- `docs/hazlenz/current/HAZLENZ_INVARIANTS.md` — the rules that hold
- `docs/hazlenz/current/BRAND-COMPATIBILITY-REGISTER.md` — which legacy identifiers survive, and why
- the repository `README.md` — how to run it
